Material Characterization

Using Atomic Force Microscopy we visualize the topography of nanoparticles and by taking force vs distance curves we have determined the Young's modulus of a wide range of nanoparticles: silica-based, polymer-based, lipid-based, and nanocrystals. The integrated AFM-FTIR system is used for determining the chemical composition by getting FTIR spectrum. Read publication here.

Tools used: Cypher S AFM, Cypher ES AFM, MFP 3D Bio AFM, Neaspec NanoFTIR

Piezoresistive temperature (GO+PEDOT:PSS) and pressure (PDMS with hemispherical microstructures) sensors were electrically charcterized to determine sensor performace characteristics. Read publication here.

Tools used: Keithley DMM6500 Digital Multimeter, HIOKI LCR meter

A porous PDMS with hemispehrical microstrcture piezoresistive pressure was electrically charcterized for performance in the low-pressure application range. Read publication here.

Tools used: Keithley DMM6500 Digital Multimeter, HIOKI LCR meter

Using Field emission scanning electron microsopcy (FESEM) and energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) we chemically characterized three different variations of polymers to compare with our own developed mixture. Read publication here.

Tools used: FESEM, EDX

FESEM, EDX and optical microscopy analysis of fabricated pressure sensor to determine chemical composition and dimensions of sensor. Read publication here.

Tools used: FESEM, EDX, optical microscope

Tools used: Solidworks, Formlabs 3B+ SLA printer, Creality Ender 3 FDM printer

Image-based analysis of image of fingernails to identify markers for predicition of anemia. A custom-phone app created to perform functionalities. Read publication here.

Tools used: MATLAB, Android Studio

Electrochemical

Developed an electrochromic covalent organic framework thin film and applied it for electronic device safety indication. As voltage changes the thin film color changes and by using the custom-made phone app and clicking a picture of the sensor we can determine the voltage in the system. Read publication here.

Tools used: Circuit design, Android studio

An electrochemical sensing platform with a specific chemical mixture to accurately determine glucose content in fluids. Read publication here.

Tools used: Electrochemical and electrical impedance spectroscopy.
